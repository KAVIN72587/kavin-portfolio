# Blog keyword plan — kavinarulraj.com

Audience: small-business owners in Chennai / India hiring for design, web and video, plus buyers comparing freelancers on Upwork/Fiverr.
Voice: first person (Kavin), plain, practical, no jargon, British spelling ("colour"), rupee figures. No invented client stories presented as real.
Every post links to 1 relevant service page + 1 pricing page + 1 older post, and ends with the yellow "hire me" CTA.

Research sources (Sep 2026): Google results/PAA for India cost queries; webdesignerforum.co.uk and LinkedIn threads on vanished web designers; Fiverr community threads on resold/copied logos; Canva help + trademark attorney posts; freelancer.in reels-editing job budgets; Hostinger / upgrowth India cost guides.

**October 2026 focus: Video editing + Website design only.** Rows 1–31 are one per day for October (row 1 = Oct 1), alternating Web and Video. Do not pick rows from the "Later" table until every October row is done.

Status: `done` = published, `queued YYYY-MM-DD` = already written and waiting in `blog-plan/queue/` to auto-publish on that date.

**Queue (added 2026-10-01):** rows 2–31 were written in advance as `blog-plan/queue/src/NN-slug.js`. `node blog-plan/queue/build.js` renders and validates them all; `node blog-plan/queue/publish-next.js` moves the post due today into the site (post file, blog.html card + count, sitemap, plan row → done). The daily task runs that, then pushes, deploys, checks the live link and pings IndexNow. To change a queued post, edit its src file and re-run build.js. Unpublished posts live outside the site folder, so deploys never leak them early.

Extra research for October (Sep 30 2026): r/VideoEditor_forhire and Upwork job posts (per-video YouTube rates), Fiverr wedding-editing gigs (teaser/highlight/full-film lengths and turnarounds), Fiverr/Upwork 2026 demand reports (podcast-to-shorts, long-form YouTube), India landing-page/clinic/restaurant website guides, small-business articles on broken contact forms and WhatsApp buttons.

## Publishing checklist (per post)
1. Create `Portfolio redesign and SEO structure/blog/<slug>.html` using an existing post as the template (same header/footer/CTA, BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, canonical `https://kavinarulraj.com/blog/<slug>/`, datePublished = today).
2. Add a card at the TOP of the list in `blog.html` (correct `data-cat`, "N min · Mon YYYY") and update the "N posts" count.
3. Add `<url>` to `sitemap.xml` under the blog block with today's lastmod.
4. Mark the row `done` here and the next row `next`.
5. Commit `Add blog post: <title>` and `git push origin main` (backup only — GitHub pushes do NOT deploy).
5b. Deploy: run `vercel --prod --yes` from `Portfolio redesign and SEO structure/`, then confirm the post URL returns 200.
6. Ping IndexNow with the new URL (key file: `2cc46bbdb1bdeb92ca5fcb9a7a2bce15.txt`).
7. After 30 posts, re-run keyword research and extend this table.
