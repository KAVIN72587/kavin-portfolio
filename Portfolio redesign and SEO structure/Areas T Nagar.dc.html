Designing for a crowded street

On Ranganathan Street your poster competes with forty others, all shouting. Being louder is not available — everyone already is.

  • One number, enormous

    The discount, the price or the date. Set it far larger than feels comfortable and cut everything competing with it.

  • Contrast beats colour

    When every neighbour is red and yellow, a dark board with one bright element is what the eye lands on.

  • Tamil set properly

    Not English typography with Tamil squeezed underneath. Both languages planned into the layout, weighted equally.
