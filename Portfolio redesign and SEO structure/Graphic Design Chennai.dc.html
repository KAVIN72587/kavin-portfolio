How a project runs

  1. STEP 01 · DAY 1

    Brief call

    Twenty minutes on what you sell, who buys it and where the design will appear.

  2. STEP 02 · DAY 2–3

    First drafts

    Concepts shown in context — on a card, a shelf, a feed — not floating on white.

  3. STEP 03 · DAY 4–6

    Two revision rounds

    Consolidated feedback, one direction taken forward and tightened.

  4. STEP 04 · DAY 7

    Files & press check

    Source files, print PDFs and web exports. I'll talk to your printer if needed.
