What an identity project includes

  • The mark

    Three concepts, one refined, drawn as vectors and tested at both extremes of scale.

  • Colour & type

    A small palette with print references, and typefaces that have a Tamil counterpart where needed.

  • Applications

    Card, signage, packaging, invoice, social profile — whichever ones your business actually uses.

  • Usage rules

    Short enough that your team reads it: minimum size, clear space, and what not to do.
