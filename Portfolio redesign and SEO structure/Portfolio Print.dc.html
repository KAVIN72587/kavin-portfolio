What ships with every print job

Most reprints in this city are caused by file problems rather than design problems. These four things prevent almost all of them.

  • CMYK with bleed

    3mm bleed, crop marks, fonts outlined. Colour converted deliberately, not by the printer's software guessing.

  • Built to their spec

    Your printer's die line, paper and finish confirmed before layout starts, not after.

  • A screen version

    RGB, compressed, hyperlinked. The same document as an email attachment that opens on a phone.

  • Press follow-up

    I speak to your printer directly and check the proof. If they flag a file issue, I fix it at no charge.
