Logo design cost in Chennai

Quotes in this city run from ₹1,500 to well over ₹1,00,000 for what sounds like the same job. The gap is almost never about talent. Here is what actually sits behind each number.

Short answer

₹8,000 – ₹22,000

What most small and mid-sized Chennai businesses pay a freelancer for a logo they can use everywhere.

Six things that move the price

  • Number of applications

    A mark alone is one job. A mark plus stationery, signage, packaging and social templates is four more.

  • Bilingual lockups

    A Tamil version isn't a translation — it's a second piece of lettering drawn to match the first.

  • Custom lettering

    Setting an existing typeface well is quick. Drawing letterforms from scratch takes days and costs accordingly.

  • Number of decision-makers

    One owner approving is fast. A committee of five means more rounds, and rounds are the real cost in design.

  • Deadline

    Anything that pushes other work down the queue carries a 25% surcharge. A comfortable date costs nothing extra.

  • Guidelines document

    A one-page usage sheet is included above ₹14,000. A full brand book is a separate piece of work.

Cost questions

Why is ₹1,500 for a logo a bad deal?+

At that price the mark is almost always a stock icon set in a common typeface, sold to other businesses too. You usually get a PNG and no vector, which means it cannot be cut in vinyl or embroidered without being redrawn — a cost you pay later.

Is a more expensive logo actually better?+

Not automatically. Above roughly ₹25,000 you are mostly paying for scope and process — more applications, more stakeholders, research and documentation — not for a better-drawn mark. Decide which of those you actually need.

What is included in your ₹8,000 package?+

Three original concepts, two revision rounds, and the full file pack: AI and SVG vectors, PNG with transparency, JPG, favicon, plus black, white and single-colour versions. Delivered in five working days.

Do I pay extra for the source files?+

No. Editable source files transfer to you on final payment, always. If anyone quotes you a separate charge to release working files, treat that as a warning.

Can I pay in instalments?+

Fifty per cent books the slot and the balance is due on delivery of final files. For identity systems above ₹22,000 I split it into three payments across the project.

What does a redesign cost compared with a new logo?+

Usually the same or slightly less, because part of the direction already exists. It is rarely cheaper by much — the work is in deciding what to keep, and that takes judgement rather than time.
