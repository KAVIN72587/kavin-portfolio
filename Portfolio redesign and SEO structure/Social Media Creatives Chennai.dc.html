What I don't do

Worth being clear, because social media is sold as a bundle by most people who offer it.

  • –

    Posting and scheduling

    Files arrive named and ready. You or your team upload them — you know your timing better than I do.

  • –

    Replying to comments and DMs

    Community management is a full-time job and should be done by someone who knows your business.

  • –

    Ad campaigns and media buying

    I design the creative. Running the spend needs a performance marketer, and I'll happily work alongside yours.

    Follower growth promises

    Nobody can promise that honestly. What I can promise is that your feed will look like one business.
