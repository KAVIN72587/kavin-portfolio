Logo design FAQs

How much does a logo cost in Chennai?+

₹8,000 for the Starter package, ₹14,000 with stationery and a social kit, ₹22,000 and up for a full identity system. GST is extra and rush work adds 25%.

How long does it take?+

First concepts in three working days, final files usually inside a week. Identity systems run two to three weeks depending on how many applications you need.

What if I don't like any of the concepts?+

It has not happened yet, but if none of the three land, I redraw a fresh set once at no charge after a second brief call. Beyond that we part ways and you pay only the booking amount.

Do I own the logo?+

Yes. Full ownership transfers on final payment, along with the vector source files. Nothing stays licensed to me.

Can you redesign an existing logo?+

Often the better option. I keep whatever equity your customers already recognise — usually the colour or the shape — and fix the parts that break in small sizes or in print.

Do you register the trademark?+

I do not file it, but I design with registrability in mind and can hand the vector files straight to your trademark agent in the format they need.
