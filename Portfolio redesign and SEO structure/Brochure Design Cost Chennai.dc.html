What changes the design price

  1. FACTOR 01

    Whether the content exists

    Finished copy and usable photographs mean the per-page rate holds. "We'll write it as we go" is the single most common reason a brochure takes six weeks instead of two, and the extra rounds get billed.

  2. FACTOR 02

    Repetition across pages

    Twelve product pages sharing one layout cost less per page than twelve pages that each need their own structure. Tell me the page mix and the quote reflects it.

  3. FACTOR 03

    Photography and illustration

    Retouching your existing images is included within reason. Stock licensing, a product shoot or custom diagrams are quoted separately because they are separate work.

  4. FACTOR 04

    Second language

    A Tamil edition is roughly 60% of the original design fee. Tamil text runs longer than English, so the layout is rebuilt rather than swapped, and it is far cheaper if planned from the start.
