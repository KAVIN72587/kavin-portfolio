Where channels lose viewers

Retention graphs fail in predictable places, and almost all of them are editing decisions rather than content problems.

  • 0:00 – 0:15

    The intro animation

    A ten-second branded intro is ten seconds of people leaving. Open on the payoff, brand it later.

  • 0:15 – 0:45

    The long preamble

    "Before we start, make sure to subscribe." Say it at the point where you have earned it.

  • MID-VIDEO

    Dead air between points

    Pauses, restarts and thinking time. Cutting these is unglamorous and does more than any effect.

  • MID-VIDEO

    No visual change

    Three minutes on one framing. B-roll, zoom shifts or on-screen text every few seconds hold attention.

  • MID-VIDEO

    Inconsistent audio

    Volume jumps between clips make people leave faster than poor picture quality does.

  • LAST 30 SEC

    The trailing goodbye

    End on the last useful thing, with the next video on screen. Long sign-offs waste the best moment to send someone onward.
