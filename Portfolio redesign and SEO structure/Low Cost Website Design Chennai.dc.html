When to spend more instead

A one-page site is right for a lot of businesses, and wrong for these. Better you know now.

  • You sell online

    Carts, payments and stock need an ecommerce build, from ₹45,000.

  • You want to rank for many searches

    Each service needs its own page. That is a multi-page site, from ₹20,000.

  • Your team publishes regularly

    A blog or news section needs a CMS behind it, not a single hand-built page.

  • You have a large catalogue

    Fifty products will not fit on one page in any way a customer can navigate.
