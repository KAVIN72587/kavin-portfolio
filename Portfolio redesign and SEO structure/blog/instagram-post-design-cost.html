What Instagram post design should cost

Social media design quotes in India vary as much as logo quotes. Some freelancers offer posts for ₹200 each. Agencies charge ₹25,000 or more a month. Monthly packages promise "30 posts" without explaining what that includes. For a small business trying to post regularly, it is hard to know what is reasonable.

Here is how Instagram and social media design is usually priced, what a good monthly package should include, what typically costs extra and how to choose the right level for your business.

01

Per-post pricing

Some designers charge per post. Very low per-post prices usually mean template edits: your logo and text dropped into a ready-made design, with little thought about your brand. Higher per-post prices usually mean original layouts, better typography and more care.

Per-post pricing suits businesses that post occasionally or need one-off creatives for an event or launch.

02

Monthly packages

Most businesses that post regularly choose a monthly package. It gives the designer time to plan a consistent look and gives you a predictable cost. My social media packs start at ₹9,000 a month for 12 post designs and 4 story templates, including festival creatives and editable templates.

Monthly work also becomes more efficient over time. Once the style is set, the designer can focus on ideas and quality rather than reinventing the look every week.

03

What a good package includes

A clear number of posts and stories per month. A content calendar or at least a monthly plan agreed in advance. Designs in your brand colours, fonts and style. Festival creatives for relevant occasions. Revision rounds per post or per batch. Final files in the right sizes for each platform.

Some packages also include captions, hashtags and scheduling. Others include only design. Make sure you know which.

Twelve well-planned posts usually do more for a small business than thirty rushed ones. Frequency matters, but consistency and quality matter more.

04

What usually costs extra

Reels and video editing are usually separate from post design, because they take much more time. Paid ad creatives in multiple sizes and variations may be extra. Product photography, copywriting for long captions, community management and running ads are separate services.

Printed versions of social posts, such as counter posters, are often available at a small extra cost because the design already exists.

05

How to choose the right level

Start with your goals and capacity. If you can post three times a week consistently, a package with 12 posts a month fits. If you are just starting, fewer posts with editable templates may suit you better.

Look at the designer's past social media work. Does it look consistent? Does each brand look different from the others? Do the posts communicate clearly at a glance on a phone?

06

What to provide

Good social media design depends on good input: photos of your products, shop and team; offers and news each month; and quick feedback. A short monthly call or message with upcoming plans makes the content far more relevant than generic posts.

Pay for consistency, not just quantity

The real value of a social media designer is not the number of posts, but a feed that looks consistent, communicates clearly and supports your actual sales and events. A modest package done well beats a large one filled with generic content.

Twelve posts with a plan beat thirty without one.

Review results every three months: which posts brought enquiries, which got saved or shared. Adjust the mix with your designer based on what works.

Questions to ask before choosing a package

  1. Quantity. How many posts and stories per month?
  2. Planning. Is there a monthly content plan?
  3. Originality. Original designs or edited templates?
  4. Extras. Are captions, reels, ads and festival posts included?
  5. Revisions. How many changes per post or batch?
  6. Files. Will I receive editable templates?

Get the answers in writing. Monthly work runs smoothly when both sides know exactly what each month includes.

Many designers offer a short trial month or a smaller starter package. It is a sensible way to test how well you work together before committing to a longer arrangement, and it gives both sides time to settle the style before the volume increases.

Be wary of packages that promise a very high number of posts at a very low price. Someone has to plan, design and check each one, and when the numbers do not add up, the quality usually drops first. It is better to agree on fewer posts that are genuinely useful than to fill your feed with filler.

Quick answers

How much does Instagram post design cost in India?

Per-post rates range from a few hundred rupees for template edits to more for original designs. Monthly packages for small businesses often start around ₹9,000 for about 12 posts plus stories.

Is a monthly social media package better than paying per post?

For regular posting, yes. Monthly packages give consistency, planning and usually a lower cost per post.

Are reels included in social media design packages?

Usually not. Reels involve video editing and are typically priced separately or added as an extra.
