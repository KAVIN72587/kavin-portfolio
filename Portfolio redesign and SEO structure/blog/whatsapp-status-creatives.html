WhatsApp status that brings enquiries

For many local businesses, WhatsApp status reaches more real customers than Instagram ever will. Everyone who has saved your number can see it, and many of them check status updates several times a day. A clothing shop posting new arrivals, a bakery showing today's cakes or a salon sharing an offer can get direct replies within minutes.

Yet most business statuses are blurry forwarded images or long text screenshots. Here is how to design status updates that people notice, understand in a second and reply to.

01

Use the full vertical size

WhatsApp status is shown full-screen on phones, in a vertical 9:16 shape. Design at 1080 × 1920 pixels so your image fills the screen without black bars. The same size works for Instagram and Facebook stories, so one design can be used everywhere.

Keep important text away from the very top and bottom, where your name, time and reply box appear.

02

Make it readable in one second

Status updates are viewed quickly, often tapped through in a few seconds. Use one clear message per status: a large photo, a short headline and one key detail like a price or offer. Big, bold text with strong contrast works better than small detailed text.

If you have more to say, split it across two or three statuses rather than cramming it into one.

03

What to post

New arrivals and today's specials, offers with an end date, behind-the-scenes moments, customer reviews, before-and-after results, event reminders, festival greetings and quick tips. Real photos of your products and shop usually get more replies than polished stock images.

Mix selling posts with useful or friendly ones, so customers do not feel they are only seeing adverts.

Add a clear next step: "Reply to order", "Call to book" or "Visit today". People are more likely to act when told exactly what to do.

04

Keep your brand consistent

Use your logo, colours and fonts on status designs, especially offers and announcements. A few simple templates in your brand style make posting quick and keep everything recognisable. Customers start to notice your updates among dozens of others.

05

How often to post

A few statuses a day is plenty for most businesses. Posting twenty in a row can annoy people and make them mute you. Posting at times when customers are likely to check, such as mornings, lunchtime and evenings, increases views.

06

Grow the audience

Only people who have saved your number can see your status. Encourage customers to save it: print it clearly on bills, packaging and visiting cards, and ask customers in person. A small sign at the counter saying "Save our number for daily offers" can grow your status audience steadily.

Small screen, direct results

WhatsApp status is one of the most direct marketing channels a local business has. People who see it already know you, and replying is one tap away. Clear, branded, vertical designs with one message and a next step turn those views into conversations and sales.

One status, one message, one clear next step.

Check who viewed your statuses and which ones got replies. Over a few weeks, you will learn what your customers respond to most.

WhatsApp status checklist

  1. Size. 1080 × 1920 vertical, text away from top and bottom.
  2. Message. One idea per status, readable in a second.
  3. Photos. Real products and shop, bright and clear.
  4. Brand. Logo, colours and fonts on templates.
  5. Action. Reply, call or visit, clearly stated.
  6. Frequency. A few a day, at times customers check.

Design a set of status templates once and you can post professional updates every day in minutes.

Status updates also work well alongside WhatsApp broadcasts. Use status for light, frequent updates that people can glance at, and save broadcasts for important offers or announcements that deserve a direct message. Using both carefully keeps customers informed without filling their chats, which is the fastest way to get muted or blocked.

Short videos work well on status too. A ten-second clip of a cake being decorated, fabric being unfolded or a dish being served often gets more replies than a still photo. Keep them short, bright and steady, add a line of text with the price or offer, and end with a clear way to order.

Finally, respect your audience. Post things they will genuinely find useful or interesting, and they will keep watching.

Over time, a steady rhythm of useful status updates becomes one of the cheapest and most reliable ways to stay in front of loyal customers.

Quick answers

What size is a WhatsApp status image?

Use a vertical image of 1080 × 1920 pixels (9:16) to fill the screen without black bars.

How many WhatsApp statuses should a business post per day?

A few a day is usually enough. Too many in a row can lead customers to mute your updates.

Who can see my WhatsApp Business status?

People who have saved your number in their contacts, depending on your privacy settings.
