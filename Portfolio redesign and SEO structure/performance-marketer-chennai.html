ChatGPT ads in India: what Chennai businesses should know

When it opened. OpenAI opened self-serve buying of ChatGPT ads through its Ads Manager to advertisers in India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa on 31 August 2026 (OpenAI announcement). Before that, ads were limited to large advertisers by invitation.

Who sees them. Ads appear for adult users on the free and Go plans. Paid Plus, Pro and business plans are ad-free.

How they look. Ads are clearly labelled as sponsored and shown separately from ChatGPT's answer. OpenAI says ads do not change what ChatGPT answers.

How targeting works. Ads are matched to the context of the current conversation. Advertisers never see anyone's chats. Product feeds, location targeting and custom audiences are available.

How you pay. Most campaigns bid on cost per click or on outcomes such as leads and purchases. Reported minimum daily budgets in India start at around ₹725. Conversions are tracked with OpenAI's pixel and Conversions API.

Restricted topics. Expect limits around health, mental health and politics, and no ads to under-18 users. Clinics and hospitals should check eligibility before planning a budget.

My take. It is early, the inventory is cheap compared with where it will be, and almost no local competitor is bidding yet. For most Chennai businesses it is a test channel next to Google or Meta, not a replacement.

Performance marketing FAQs

How much does a performance marketer cost in Chennai?+

My management fee starts at ₹25,000 a month for one ad channel — Meta, Google, LinkedIn or ChatGPT. Each extra channel is quoted on scope. Ad spend is separate and paid by you directly to the platform. For comparison, Chennai freelancers usually quote ₹8,000–₹30,000 a month and agencies ₹25,000–₹75,000 a month before ad spend.

Is ad spend included in the ₹25,000?+

No. The ₹25,000 is my fee for strategy, setup, creatives and management. Ad spend goes on your own card or invoice in your own ad account, so you see every rupee the platform charges and nothing is marked up.

Should I start with Meta ads or Google ads?+

If people already search for what you sell — a dentist in Velachery, a plumber in Anna Nagar, a 2BHK in OMR — start with Google Search, because you are catching demand that already exists. If you sell something people do not search for yet, such as a new D2C product or an event, start with Meta, because it creates demand by showing the offer to the right people. Many businesses end up running both.

Are LinkedIn ads worth it for a Chennai business?+

Only if you sell to businesses and one customer is worth a lot — IT services, SaaS, industrial suppliers, recruitment, B2B training. LinkedIn clicks often cost ₹150–₹650 in India, so it is wasted on low-ticket consumer products. For a ₹5 lakh contract, a ₹2,000 lead is cheap.

What are ChatGPT ads and can Chennai businesses run them?+

ChatGPT ads are sponsored placements shown inside ChatGPT, clearly labelled and kept separate from the answer. OpenAI opened self-serve buying through its Ads Manager to advertisers in India on 31 August 2026. Targeting is based on the context of the conversation, advertisers do not see anyone's chats, and bidding is mainly cost per click or outcome-optimised. Yes, a Chennai business can run them today.

How long before paid ads show results?+

Google Search can bring enquiries in the first week because it catches people already searching. Meta and LinkedIn need two to four weeks of data before the platforms learn who converts. I plan in 90-day blocks: month one to set up and learn, month two to cut what is not working, month three to put more money behind what is.

Do you design the ad creatives as well?+

Yes, and that is the main difference from most performance marketers. I am also a graphic designer and video editor, so static ads, carousels and short video ads are made in-house and refreshed when they tire, without waiting on a separate designer.

Who owns the ad accounts and the data?+

You do. Ad accounts, Business Manager, Google Ads, Analytics and pixels are created in your name and I am added as a partner. If we stop working together, you remove my access and keep everything, including the campaign history.

Do you guarantee leads or a fixed ROAS?+

No. Anyone who guarantees a number before seeing your offer, price and sales process is guessing. What I commit to is a clear plan, honest weekly numbers — cost per lead, cost per qualified lead, return on ad spend — and fast decisions to stop money going into what does not work.

Is there a minimum contract?+

Three months. Ad platforms need time and data to learn, and judging a campaign on its first two weeks usually means stopping just before it starts working. After three months it runs month to month.
