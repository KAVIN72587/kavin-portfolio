Where Indian stores lose the sale

Cart abandonment here has specific causes, and most of them are fixable in the build rather than with discounts afterwards.

  • Shipping cost revealed late

    A ₹90 delivery charge appearing at step three feels like a trick. Show it on the product page.

  • Forced account creation

    Guest checkout with a phone number is enough. Nobody wants a password for a one-off purchase.

  • No COD option

    Still a large share of orders outside metro cores. Offer it, with a small fee if the margin needs it.

  • Pincode uncertainty

    A pincode check on the product page answers "will it even reach me" before the cart.

  • Payment failure dead-end

    UPI drops happen. The cart must survive it and offer another method instead of emptying.

  • Return policy hidden

    First-time buyers look for it before paying. Put it beside the button, not in the footer.
