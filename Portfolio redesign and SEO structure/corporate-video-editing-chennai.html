Cut it to the point

The most valuable thing an editor does on corporate work is remove material the company is attached to. Founder's history, every department mentioned, the full award list — all of it costs you the viewer before the point arrives.

Nobody has ever complained that a company video was too short.

  • One video, one job

    A film that introduces the company, sells the product and recruits staff does none of the three. Two shorter videos beat one long one.

  • The first eight seconds

    Logo animations belong at the end. Open on the thing that makes someone stay.

  • Watched without sound

    On LinkedIn and in a lobby, audio is off. Subtitles are burnt in and key numbers appear on screen.

Working with a marketing team

  • Brand kit up front

    Fonts, colours and logo files at the start. Lower thirds and titles then match your other material exactly.

  • One approver

    Feedback consolidated by one person. Six people sending contradictory notes is what makes edits take three weeks.

  • Timecoded feedback

    Review links where comments attach to the frame. Far faster than a document describing a moment in words.

  • NDA and confidentiality

    Signed on request before any footage moves. Some work never appears in my portfolio, which is normal and fine.

  • Invoicing and GST

    Proper invoices with PO references, matched to your finance team's process and payment cycle.

  • Source files on request

    Project files handed over if your team needs to re-version later. No holding your footage hostage.

Corporate video FAQs

How much does corporate video editing cost in Chennai?+

₹8,000 to ₹14,000 per finished video depending on type. A company profile is ₹12,000 and an animated explainer ₹14,000. For ongoing volume, ₹35,000 a month covers up to eight videos.

Do you shoot as well?+

No, editing only. If you need a crew I can recommend people I have worked with in Chennai, billed by them directly. Plenty of clients also send footage shot in-house, which is fine.

How long does an edit take?+

First cut in five working days for a single video, faster for event recaps where speed is the point. Two revision rounds are included and usually take another three or four days.

Will you sign an NDA?+

Yes, before any footage moves. Some corporate work never appears in my portfolio for that reason, which is normal — I will show you comparable work under similar terms instead.

Can you match our existing brand guidelines?+

Send the brand kit at the start — fonts, colours, logo files, and an existing video if there is one. Lower thirds and titles are then built to match rather than approximated afterwards.

We need it in Tamil and English.+

Two subtitle tracks or two full versions with separate voiceovers, depending on the audience. Subtitles are the cheaper option and work for most internal and LinkedIn use.
