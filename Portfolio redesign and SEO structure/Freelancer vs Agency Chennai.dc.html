When you should hire the agency

Four situations where I'd tell you to go elsewhere, because pretending otherwise wastes your money and my reputation.

  • Several things at once, on the same date

    A launch needing a film crew, a photoshoot, packaging and a site in the same four weeks needs parallel teams. I work in sequence.

  • You need media buying and strategy

    I design and edit. I don't plan campaigns, buy ad space or run performance marketing, and I won't pretend to.

  • Procurement requires a vendor panel

    Larger companies often need registered vendors, insurance cover and a legal entity on the invoice. That's a fair requirement and an agency meets it.

  • The work must never pause

    If a daily output can't stop when one person is ill or travelling, you need a team with cover built in.

Where the money actually goes

An agency quote isn't inflated for no reason — it carries costs I don't have. Whether that's worth paying depends entirely on your project.
