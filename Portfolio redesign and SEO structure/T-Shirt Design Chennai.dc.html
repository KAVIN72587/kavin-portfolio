Things people forget

  • Print size, not image size

    A chest print is usually 10 to 12 inches wide. Artwork gets built at that size, not scaled up from a logo file.

  • Placement height

    Two inches below the collar seam reads right on a person. Centred on the flat garment sits too low.

  • Garment colour first

    Pick the tee shade before the artwork. Designing on white and printing on charcoal changes everything.

  • Size range

    One print size across S to XXL looks small on the big sizes. Two scaled versions solve it.

  • Wash durability

    Large solid areas crack faster than open designs. Worth knowing before approving a full-chest block.

  • Sleeve and back options

    A small sleeve mark often does more for a uniform than a bigger chest print.
