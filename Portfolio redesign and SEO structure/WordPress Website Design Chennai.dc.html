Why cheap theme sites go wrong

Nothing wrong with WordPress. The problem is how it usually gets used — a heavy multipurpose theme, fifteen plugins to cover its gaps, and nobody responsible once it's handed over.

  • Plugin sprawl

    Twenty plugins means twenty things that can break on an update, and every one slows the site down.

  • Theme lock-in

    Content built inside a theme's page builder is stuck there. Change theme and your pages come out as soup.

  • Unoptimised images

    A 4MB photo straight from the camera on the homepage. The single most common reason a site feels slow.

  • Security left open

    Default admin URL, no updates for two years, no backups. This is how sites end up serving spam.

  • Nobody trained

    Changing a phone number becomes a paid support request, so it never gets changed.

  • No access handover

    Domain and hosting registered in the developer's name. You do not own your own website.

Everything on your site is registered in your name, with the logins in your inbox. If you stop working with me, nothing needs to move.
