What goes into one reel

Six things, every time. None of them optional, none of them charged separately.

01

A hook in the first second

The strongest frame goes first, even if it happened last. Chronology is not a structure.

02

Captions built for mute

Burned in, timed to speech, and positioned clear of the UI so nothing sits under the like button.

03

Sound design

Licensed music, cleaned dialogue and cuts placed on the beat so the pacing feels deliberate.

04

Colour

Graded in Resolve so phone footage from three different days looks like one shoot.

05

Three crops

9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 from the same edit, reframed properly rather than letterboxed.

06

A cover frame

A designed thumbnail so your grid looks composed instead of like a wall of random stills.
