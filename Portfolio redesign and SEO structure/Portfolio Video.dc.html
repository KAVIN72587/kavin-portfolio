How these were cut

The footage decides how long it takes. The brief decides how long it should be.

  • Audio first

    A line of speech or a vow carries the structure. Music is chosen to fit that, not the other way round.

  • Shorter than asked

    Almost every brief here was delivered under its target length. Nobody has objected yet.

  • Subtitled by hand

    Machine captions mangle names and Tamil words. Every caption here was corrected manually.

  • Licensed music

    No film songs. A claimed or muted video is worse than a plainer track nobody notices.
