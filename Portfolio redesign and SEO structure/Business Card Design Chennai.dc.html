Six ways cards go wrong at the printer

All six are avoidable in the file. Most cheap card designs contain at least two.

  • Text too close to the edge

    Guillotines drift by a millimetre or two. Anything inside that margin gets clipped on some cards in the stack.

  • Phone numbers at 6pt

    If your customer needs their glasses to dial you, the design has failed regardless of how it looks.

  • Rich black gone muddy

    Black set as 100% K prints grey on coated stock. It needs a proper CMYK build.

  • Spot UV without a mask

    The printer needs a separate layer marking exactly where the gloss goes. Without it they guess.

  • A logo supplied as PNG

    At card size a screen-resolution logo goes soft. It has to be vector.

  • Fonts not outlined

    The press machine substitutes whatever it has, and nobody spots it until 1,000 cards are cut.
